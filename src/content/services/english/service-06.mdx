## IP-1 Licensing & Sharing Overview

As a licensed Infrastructure Provider Category-I, PCN Communication Private Limited owns, constructs, maintains, and leases passive telecommunication infrastructure assets to licensed communication service providers.

We enable mobile operators, internet service providers, and digital network managers to transition from expensive capital expenditure models toward efficient operational expenditure models. By sharing passive assets such as fiber routes, tower footprints, and equipment shelters, our clients can expand their network footprints rapidly while reducing capital costs and environmental impact.

## Asset Protection & Operational Reliability

PCN Communication maintains complete operational responsibility for all passive infrastructure assets within our IP-1 portfolio. Our dedicated field security and maintenance teams conduct twenty-four-hour physical patrols along dark fiber routes and around tower installations to prevent third-party construction damage or unauthorized access.

We guarantee continuous power availability for active electronics housed within our telecom shelters through automated backup power systems. Furthermore, all our IP-1 operations adhere strictly to national telecommunication regulatory frameworks, ensuring legal, physical, and operational compliance for all tenant operators.
