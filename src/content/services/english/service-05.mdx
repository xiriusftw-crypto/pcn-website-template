## Civil Fiber Construction Overview

Optical Fiber Cables serve as the foundational backbone for modern high-speed telecommunication networks. PCN Communication Private Limited provides full-scale civil engineering and underground fiber construction services, building robust physical cable routes across urban highways, crowded metropolitan streets, and remote rural corridors.

By combining advanced trenchless technology with traditional civil excavation methods, we create long-lasting underground conduit networks that protect fiber assets from physical disruption, weather extremes, and surface construction activities.

## Pneumatic Blowing, Fusion Splicing & Testing

Once underground conduits are secured, our technical teams install optical fiber cables using high-pressure pneumatic blowing equipment. This technique floats fiber cables through pre-laid High-Density Polyethylene sub-ducts over long continuous distances, eliminating mechanical pulling friction and protecting delicate optical strands.

Following cable placement, our certified fiber technicians perform core-alignment fusion splicing to join cable segments, maintaining optical attenuation loss well below industry standards. Every fiber route undergoes rigorous testing using Optical Time-Domain Reflectometers, Chromatic Dispersion analyzers, and optical power meters to verify link performance before final network commissioning.
