As mobile data consumption expands and cellular networks transition toward widespread 5G coverage, robust passive infrastructure is critical to maintaining wireless network connectivity. PCN Communication Private Limited delivers turnkey telecommunications tower engineering, civil construction, and site deployment services across diverse geographic terrains. From initial site sourcing and structural civil foundations to tower fabrication, vertical erection, and power integration, we build resilient physical infrastructure designed to support multi-tenant communications equipment under severe operational conditions.

## Ground-Based & Roof-Top Tower Solutions

Our engineering division constructs a complete array of wireless tower architectures customized for specific coverage environments. For macro-cellular networks, rural connectivity drives, and highway coverage, we build heavy-duty Ground-Based Towers ranging from thirty to sixty meters in height. These structures are built upon custom-engineered reinforced concrete foundations designed around local soil mechanics and wind-load metrics. In dense urban centers where ground space is limited, PCN Communication deploys Roof-Top Towers and Monopoles. Our engineers perform structural load assessments on host buildings to ensure safe weight distribution, utilizing non-penetrating or anchored mounting systems that preserve building integrity while expanding urban micro-cell capacity.

## Turnkey Execution Lifecycle

PCN Communication manages every phase of tower deployment through a seamless execution model. Our site acquisition specialists conduct detailed land surveys, soil tests, structural audits, and legal Right-of-Way clearances before civil works begin. Once site access is secured, our civil construction teams execute foundation excavation, concrete pouring, and steel rebar installation according to precise structural blueprints. Following foundation curing, steel lattice sections are erected using specialized hoisting machinery, followed by verticality checks and bolt-torque verification. Finally, our technical teams integrate electrical grid connections, commercial backup generators, hybrid solar power systems, aviation hazard lighting, perimeter fencing, and earthing protection.
